# Advantech ADAM-6050

18-channel isolated digital I/O on Modbus TCP: 12 DI, 6 DO. Map from Appendix B (pp. 204-208). DI are read as coils (0X), not discrete inputs. Counters are two registers with the low word first, the opposite of the ioLogik's.

The manuals are listed in `collect/` with their hashes; they are not in this repository.
