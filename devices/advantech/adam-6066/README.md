# Advantech ADAM-6066

6-channel digital input (3 kHz counter / frequency, latch) and 6 Form A power relay outputs (250 VAC at 5 A, 30 VDC at 3 A), 10/100BASE-T, Modbus/TCP (section 5.6, pp. 45-46). The mapping table is shared with the ADAM-6060 (Appendix B.2, pp. 217-219).

The manuals are listed in `collect/` with their hashes; they are not in this repository.
