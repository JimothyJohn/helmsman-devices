# Siemens SIMATIC ET 200SP IM 155-6 MF HF

The ET 200SP interface module that can be PROFINET, EtherNet/IP or Modbus TCP device (shared between up to six controllers: two of each, webinar p. 18). Its EtherNet/IP integration file is a generic EDS and a UDT exported from the MultiFieldbus Configuration Tool (pp. 15 and 26); Modbus TCP register layouts are exported there too.

The equipment manual (6ES7155-6MU01-0CN0) and the GSDML page were refused or are JavaScript-only (see the comments in `collect/siemens.toml`; two earlier tries and one more with Siemens' webinar slides), so this folder holds the identity of the product and what the slides say, and no points. The article number is not stated in the slides read.

The slides are listed in `collect/siemens.toml` with their hash; they are not in this repository.
